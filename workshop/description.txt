[h1]Grand Central Terminal[/h1]
The Main Concourse of New York's Grand Central Terminal at true scale: the green zodiac vault, the end windows with light shafts, the double staircases and balconies, the four-faced clock on the information booth, the ticket windows, the departure board, the flag and the chandeliers.

Update 0.8.0 adds Lexington Passage: through the east floor arch, a shop-lined passage to the Lexington Avenue doors. Update 0.7.0 added the lower-level tracks: from the dining concourse, the TRACK 108 and 112 gates lead down to a second train shed under the upper one. Update 0.6.1 shipped a nav mesh, so nextbots and NPCs can walk the station. Update 0.6.0 added the Biltmore Room: step through the marble doorway at the northwest corner of the concourse into the old arrival room with its beamed ceiling, brass sconces and the old arrivals board.

Update 0.5.0 added the exterior: walk out through the Vanderbilt Hall vestibule onto 42nd Street and look up at the facade, its great arched windows, the clock and the Glory of Commerce group, then climb the terrace stair or cross the Park Avenue Viaduct.

Update 0.3.0 added Vanderbilt Hall: walk through the passage beside the ticket windows into the old waiting room with its five tiered chandeliers, gilded cornice and tall windows, and the 42nd Street doors.

Update 0.2.0 added the lower level: walk down the ramps from the concourse to the Whispering Gallery with its tiled vault, the Oyster Bar with its counters and stools, and the dining concourse with its clock booth. The platforms and the facade will follow in updates.
