[h1]Grand Central Terminal[/h1]
The Main Concourse of New York's Grand Central Terminal at true scale: the green zodiac vault, the end windows with light shafts, the double staircases and balconies, the four-faced clock on the information booth, the ticket windows, the departure board, the flag and the chandeliers.

This is the first release. The Main Concourse is done; more of the terminal (Vanderbilt Hall, the Whispering Gallery, the dining concourse, platforms) will follow in updates.

[h2]Details[/h2]
[list]
[*]Map name: gm_grand_central
[*]Two spawns: the west balcony and the concourse floor (see the level map in the screenshots)
[*]Runs at hundreds of frames per second on a mid-range PC
[*]Needs nothing else installed (Half-Life 2 textures ship with Garry's Mod)
[/list]
