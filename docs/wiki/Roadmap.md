The areas get built in this order. Each one goes through blockout, a playable compile, detail, lighting and optimisation.

1. **Main Concourse** (released in 0.1.0). It's done when a screenshot from the west balcony could pass for the real place at a glance, the map compiles with no leaks, and it holds 60 fps on a mid-range PC.
2. **Lower level** (released in 0.2.0): the dining concourse level below, with the ramps, the Whispering Gallery's Guastavino tile arches and the Oyster Bar.
3. **Vanderbilt Hall** (released in 0.3.0) and the 42nd Street entrance passage.
4. **Upper-level track platforms** (released in 0.4.0), with a static train car to walk through.
5. **The exterior** (released in 0.5.0): the 42nd Street facade with the Glory of Commerce group and its clock, the Park Avenue Viaduct, and a Midtown skybox.
6. Extras if the budget allows: lower-level tracks, the Biltmore Room (released in 0.6.0), roleplay spaces and a nav mesh.

The budgets: under about 150 MB packed for the Workshop, func_detail for decoration, areaportals between areas, and a cubemap per area.
