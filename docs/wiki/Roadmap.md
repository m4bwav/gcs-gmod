The areas get built in this order. Each one goes through blockout, a playable compile, detail, lighting and optimisation.

1. **Main Concourse** (in progress). It's done when a screenshot from the west balcony could pass for the real place at a glance, the map compiles with no leaks, and it holds 60 fps on a mid-range PC.
2. The dining concourse level below, with the ramps, the Whispering Gallery's Guastavino tile arches and the Oyster Bar.
3. Vanderbilt Hall and the 42nd Street entrance passage.
4. Upper-level track platforms, with a static train car or two to walk through.
5. The exterior: the 42nd Street facade with the Glory of Commerce group and its clock, the Park Avenue Viaduct, and a Midtown skybox.
6. Extras if the budget allows: lower-level tracks, the Biltmore Room, roleplay spaces and a nav mesh.

The budgets: under about 150 MB packed for the Workshop, func_detail for decoration, areaportals between areas, and a cubemap per area.
