## 0.2.0 (2026-11-24)

- The lower level, 22 ft below the concourse: ramps from the concourse floor at both ends, under the balconies' side arches.
- The Whispering Gallery under a true groin vault in pale herringbone Guastavino tile, with corner piers, brass handrails and a crown lamp.
- The Oyster Bar: four by four tiled groin vaults on piers, a bronze OYSTER BAR sign in the entrance, oak counters with stools and pendant lamps.
- The dining concourse: pylons, a coffered ceiling with brass lamps, lit shop fronts and a small booth with a four-faced clock.
- An east landing hall with three sealed brass doors (they will lead to the platforms).
- The level map shows both levels with labels.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-23)

- The concourse at true scale with a flattened barrel vault, five bays per long wall, pilasters, a gold cornice and a modillion band.
- Procedural textures: marble floor, limestone ashlar, the zodiac vault mural with outlined constellations, coffered arch soffits and window glass.
- The end walls: three arched windows with mullions, sun shafts, paired columns, and arcades at floor and balcony level leading to lit passages.
- The information booth with a see-through glass ring, its interior, and a four-faced opal clock.
- Double staircases with balustrades, 16 chandeliers, the departure board with a dot-matrix font, and the flag.
- Lunette hoods with voussoir relief and keystones.
- The build, screenshot and photo-compare tools. The repository went public.
