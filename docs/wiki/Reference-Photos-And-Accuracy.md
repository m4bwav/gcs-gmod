## Measured and estimated

From secondary sources (see `refs/concourse-dimensions.md`):

- The springline is about 86 ft and the vault rises about 38.75 ft above it. The map uses 85 ft and 40 ft.

Still estimated from photos:

- the bay window bottom (42 ft)
- the balcony arcade openings (14 x 14 ft), the end window sill and the window heads
- the flag: the map's is 38 x 20 ft, and the real one may be about 48 ft long
- the end windows: the map's are 26 ft wide, and the real ones may be about 33 x 60 ft
- the size of the departure board

A HABS section or a Landmarks Preservation Commission report would settle these.

## Stylised on purpose

- The constellations are stick figures over simple silhouettes, not the real ceiling's figure drawings.
- The sun shafts are fake volumetric light made from additive brushes.
